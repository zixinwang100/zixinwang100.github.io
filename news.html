Ph.D. Candidate Zixin Wang Selected as Lyles Teaching Fellow for Spring 2025

The CCE Graduate Committee has recommended Ph.D. candidate Zixin Wang for the prestigious Lyles Teaching Fellowship for Spring 2025. As a Lyles Teaching Fellow, Zixin will contribute to CE 27000: Introduction to Structural Mechanics, gaining valuable teaching experience that will enhance his preparation for a successful academic career.

During the fellowship, Zixin will delivers lectures, lead active learning sessions, assist the faculty mentor with preparing homework, quizzes, and exams, and engage in various other teaching-related activities. This unique opportunity highlights Zixin’s commitment to education and his potential as an academic leader in the field.

CE grad students place third in international structural health competition

A team of Purdue graduate students including Zixin Wang, Wen Tang, and Malleswari Kachireddy participated in the 3rd International Competition for Structural Health Monitoring (IC-SHM 2022) organized by the Asia-Pacific Network of Centers for Research in Smart Structures Technology (ANCRiSST), Tongji University, Harbin Institute of Technology, and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

45 teams from 9 counties participated in this international competition and the Purdue team received the third-place award for their project titled, "Lost Data Reconstruction and Damage Identification."
