Zixin Wang

Zixin Wang

Ph.D., M. ASCE

Zixin Wang received his Ph.D. degree in the Lyles School of Civil and Construction Engineering at Purdue University in 2025, his M.S. degree in the College of Civil Engineering at Tongji University in 2019, and his B.S. degree in the School of Civil and Water Conservancy Engineering at Shandong University in 2016. He is currently a postdoctoral research associate in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, under the supervision of Professor Mohamad Alipour. His research interests are AI-driven structural health monitoring and nondestructive evaluation, physics-informed machine learning, intelligent and resilient infrastructure systems, remote sensing, and structural vibration control.
